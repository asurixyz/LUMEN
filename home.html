LUMEN Dashboard

Illumination Through Data

This dashboard provides access to datasets visualizing the primary forces shaping the 21st-century global landscape. Organized into global, regional, maritime, and neighborhood sections with interactive visualizations.

I. Global & National Dynamics

  • Interactive Global Trade Map

    Visualizing global trade partnerships with the US and China from 2000–2022.

  • Geopolitical & Economic Futures

    Long-term projections of GDP, population, R&D, and military spending.

II. Contemporary Regional Data

  • Asian Economic Indicators

    Key economic metrics for major Asian economies and ASEAN bloc.

III. Strategic Maritime Corridors

  • South China Sea

    Trade, investments, and energy dynamics in a contested region.

  • Bay of Bengal

    Regional trade and supply chain corridor analysis.

  • East African Coast

    Trade and investment ties with Asian economies.

  • Persian Gulf

    Flows of trade and finance shaping global energy stability.

IV. India's Neighborhood

  • Afghanistan

    Post-2021 shifts in economy and security.

  • Maldives

    Tourism, debt, and development under India–China competition.

  • Sri Lanka

    Economic and military ties with India and China.

  • Bangladesh

    Socio-economic and geopolitical growth drivers.

  • Pakistan

    Economic crises and strategic dependencies.
